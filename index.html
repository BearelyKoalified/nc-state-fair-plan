    Suggested order

    1. Start with the exhibit areas: Village of Yesteryear, State Fair Ark, Children's Barnyard, Heritage Circle. They close at 9:45 PM, so do them first.
    2. Grab food along the way and at the midway.
    3. Ride the America 250 Wheel around 8:30–9 PM for the lit-up view. Buy the ticket early, since the line builds after dark.
    4. Stake out a fireworks spot by 9:40 PM. Fireworks are at 9:45 PM over the pond.
